# v1.10 — GM legacy, achievements and continuing careers

Legacy is attached to the existing GM career, rather than to a club. Home has a points/achievements shortcut; Club links to the full Legacy screen. Its three views cover the season brief and scoring ledger, permanent achievements, and jobs/contracts. Starting a completely new career still requires the existing explicit save-replacement confirmation.

## Season scoring

The target is frozen before the first game using both current starting-XI quality and club stature. All 20 club targets are retained for the management award. Targets do not change after transfers, development, injuries or results. The following components are additive:

| Result or action | Legacy points |
| --- | ---: |
| Each place above / below the season target | +20 / −20 |
| League champions | +500 |
| First title with a particular club | +150 |
| First title with an underdog you took over | +800 |
| Top four without winning the title | +100 |
| Other survival | +25 |
| Finish at least five places above the target | +75 |
| Unbeaten league season | +150 |
| 100 or more league points | +100 |
| Relegation and dismissal | −250 |
| Financial compliance overspend | −30 to −150 |
| Increase in club debt during the season | −10 to −100 |
| Club Ballon d’Or winner | +150 |
| Club Player of the Season | +75 |
| Golden Boot / Playmaker / Golden Glove | +60 each |
| Yashin / Kopa | +80 each |
| Chance Creator of the Season | +40 |
| Manager of the Season | +100 |
| Earned, higher-paying poaching move | +50 |

An underdog qualifies when the GM joined with a survival target (17th) or a starting-XI rating of 74 or lower. The entry conditions stay attached to the tenure, so later squad improvement does not remove rebuilding credit. The rebuilding/first-title bonuses are awarded once per club in the career, even after leaving and returning. This does not alter any footballer's real ability based on club membership.

Shared awards pay once per type. Player award credit requires at least half the winner's recorded season appearances at the managed club, preventing a single cameo or an offseason transfer from claiming another club's work. Manager of the Season uses actual table results relative to the frozen club targets, points and a title bonus. Its name is archived with the award, so moving jobs cannot rewrite an old winner.

Only completed, committed seasons settle. Stable ledger IDs and a settled-season list prevent duplicate points after refresh, repeat commits, award-archive visits or rollover. Achievements add no repeatable points. A negative legacy total is permitted; already unlocked achievements remain unlocked.

## Jobs, poaching and rebuilding

The end-of-season job graph shows all 20 clubs in next season's league, including the three seeded, randomly promoted clubs. Available contracts are highlighted. Established and elite managers can accept any club, including the smallest; unknown managers receive smaller-club/promoted-club opportunities and stronger opportunities following exceptional success. Earned reputation expands at 350 and 1,000 legacy points and retains existing experience.

Poaching requires meeting the completed season's target, a different employer of at least the previous club's stature, and a higher salary. Salary estimates reflect employer resources, experience, legacy and recent success. Accepting a contract requires an explicit final confirmation. It advances the same world once and starts next season, preventing immediate resign/rejoin or repeated move rewards. Staying is supported without a move bonus. The GM's salary is stored as career contract metadata; existing head-coach payroll remains in the club finance simulation, and no personal spending economy is added.

A relegated GM is dismissed but can continue the same career with a new contract. The relegated team goes into the Championship pool as before. Club moves retain the world seed, all existing player identities, club accounts, staff, records, custom rosters, managerial specialty, achievements, points and prior club attribution. The GM takes the new employer's current squad, eligible auto lineup, transfer budget and manager-control flag. Pending/old match instructions do not move to the new club. Ordinary aging, expiring contracts and the existing summer youth intake still happen at rollover.

## Achievements

There are 24 achievement definitions: first and repeat league titles; titles with three clubs; championships with the original 20 custom clubs and all 44 clubs; an underdog rebuild; relegation; exceeding expectations; an unbeaten season; 100 points; managing three clubs; a successful poaching move; 1,000 legacy points; each of the nine award types; all nine types over a career; and all nine in one season. Progress bars and a 44-club title checklist make long-term goals visible.

## Save compatibility

The save version and browser key remain unchanged. Legacy is an optional, validated extension. Existing careers adopt it without replacing players or results. Adoption after the season has started is labelled as a mid-season estimate and freezes the squad-based target at adoption. Older untracked seasons receive no invented legacy history. Historical score-only seasons remain usable; a management award can be based on results, while player awards still require actual reports. New history entries include the managed club so future job changes preserve attribution.
