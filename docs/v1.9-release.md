# v1.9 — reusable substitutions and Home form advice

The v1.8 key-moment system was verified live before this update: HTTP 200, successful GitHub Pages deployment, all six decision controls in the production bundle and a SHA-256 match against the tested build. Its default interactive Watch Match behavior remains active in v1.9. Both visible version labels now read the package version, replacing a stale mobile footer label.

## Reuse substitutions

**Matchday → Reuse most recent subs** applies the most recent usable plan without reopening the individual player/time inputs. The recent-plan disclosure previews player names, minutes, formation and available-change counts. The substitution sheet also offers **Save this plan for reuse**.

- Completed managed-club matches automatically retain their actual substitutions and bench. Explicitly saved plans are retained too, capped at five distinct patterns.
- Existing v1.7/v1.8 committed reports can supply plans immediately. Pending/uncommitted reports are excluded. The small retained history carries across season rollover even after detailed old reports are archived.
- Reuse is opt-in before kickoff. It does not change the chosen starting XI, transfer a player, alter ownership, spend money or silently schedule the same instructions every week.
- Applying a plan checks current club membership, injuries, retirement, registration, current starters, bench capacity, goalkeeper/outfield slots, re-entry restrictions, the five-player limit and allowed windows.
- Valid instructions are applied in order and invalid instructions are reported as skipped. Incoming players are prioritised when rebuilding the bench, with goalkeeper cover and available reserves filling remaining places. A completely unusable plan leaves the current plan unchanged.
- One-tap reuse chooses the newest plan with at least one usable instruction; old or partly unavailable plans remain visible for review. The usual positional penalties apply if a retained player now occupies a less familiar role.

## Player form on Home

The new **Player form** section shows **Best 3 averages** and **Lowest 3 averages** for the current squad this season. The underlying average is `sum(match rating × minutes played) / total minutes`, avoiding equal influence from a one-minute cameo and a full match. Legacy reports without minutes use their original full-match convention of 90 minutes.

Only committed match reports and appearances for the managed club count. Pending results, unused substitutes, appearances at another club, departed players and retired players do not enter the current-squad ranking. Players without appearances have no invented rating. Ties use minutes and stable player IDs; the two lists do not duplicate players when few ratings exist. A new season naturally starts a new report.

Rows show the average, appearances, minutes and starting-XI/rotation status. Fewer than three appearances or 180 minutes is labelled an early sample. Advice considers injuries, registration, fitness and positional fit before recommending a selection change. A struggling regular can receive a named replacement suggestion only when an available bench player has relevant evidence, suitable position familiarity, adequate fitness and a materially higher average. A strong team is not told to drop a good performer merely because that player's average is relatively lower.

Profiles open directly from the rows, and **Review starting XI** opens the squad screen. Advice is informational: it never changes the lineup automatically.
