# v2.0 — play the full contest

## Matchday and interface

Play Match joins Watch Match and Quick Sim, at the top of match setup. Manual contests run for 3, 4 or 6 real-time minutes, representing two complete 45-minute halves. Home has direct squad, transfer, club and performance shortcuts. The navigation-help button explains the current area and links to all six career tool groups. League and squad tabs fit on small screens; visible focus, larger action targets and clearer panels apply across the app.

Manual play uses its own uncluttered, top-down 11-a-side screen: a scoreboard, full pitch, selected-player portrait/energy, movement stick, two action buttons and quick Subs/Controls access. The main navigation stays outside the playing surface. Club colors and contrast-aware opponent kits remain active. This is original 2D gameplay, not a licensed recreation of another football game.

## Controls

- Move with the left stick. Tap a teammate on the pitch to select them.
- In possession: aim a pass toward a teammate; hold Pass to loft it. Hold Shoot for power; horizontal aim chooses the goal side.
- Out of possession: the buttons become Switch and Tackle. Timing, positioning and player skill matter.
- Keyboard: WASD/arrows, J pass/switch, K or Space shoot/tackle, Q switch, P pause.
- Pause for abilities, substitutions and help. Penalties/free kicks awarded to your side use the existing drawn-shot mini-game.
- Half time pauses for a team review, energy recovery and substitutions; the teams change ends. The assistant can finish the remaining minutes after an explicit confirmation, keeping the score and completed events.

The movement stick and action buttons have independent pointer capture for two-thumb play. Input clears on cancellation, pause, focus loss and backgrounding. Stoppages briefly buffer actions, rather than dropping a press. No watching speed multiplier affects player control.

## Authoritative football

The selected fixture begins 0–0 with no predetermined goals. Players accelerate and turn, carry the ball, support runs, mark, press and contest tackles. Passes travel through space and can be intercepted or miscontrolled; long/lofted deliveries involve aerial reach. Shots travel toward goal; keeper positioning, reactions, reach and handling can produce catches or parries. A goal requires the live shot to cross the opposing goal between the posts and below the crossbar. The game also handles offside receptions, throw-ins, goal kicks, corners after deflections, fouls, set pieces, restarts and both halves. Other nine league games retain their normal simulation results.

Detailed attributes and each footballer's assigned traits affect the relevant situations: speed/acceleration/agility, fatigue/stamina, dribbling/shielding, passing/vision/delivery, finishing/power/range, heading/jumping, defensive awareness/interception/tackling, and goalkeeper reactions/positioning/handling/distribution. Positional penalties and exclusive manager patterns still apply. All assigned traits are available through the live abilities panel; players do not acquire unrelated elite traits simply by entering manual mode. The avatar appearance overhaul is a separate following update; v2.0 retains existing illustrated portraits.

Player/opponent AI, the ball, deterministic random state, physical positions, game clock, substitutions, actual events and counters belong to the saved manual state. The result uses the same event/stat/box-score/awards/records pipeline as the existing game. Manual tackles are actual successful challenges, not generated match totals. Passing, interception and live-distance counters are retained in individual box scores and their compact season archives. Partial assistant handoffs label their player-controlled coverage instead of inventing passing statistics for simulated minutes.

## Chemistry and development

Successful forward combinations can earn extra familiarity for the actual teammates involved. A pair must progress the ball and wait between qualifying combinations; back-and-forth spam does not generate unlimited rewards. The maximum is 0.35 extra familiarity per pair and four total units per match, also bounded by their shared minutes. The bonus is applied once on the normal matchweek commit, alongside ordinary shared-minute chemistry. Existing familiarity and live combinations also help pass execution. No pass, trait or input guarantees a result.

Actual minutes, match ratings and performance still enter the existing youth-development model. Developer coaching, uncertainty, injuries, form and potential ceilings remain relevant. Manual play gives a direct way to earn productive minutes and performances; it does not bypass potential limits.

## Substitutions and saves

Prearranged and AI changes remain part of the match. Manual changes use the same nine-player bench, five substitutions and three stoppage windows plus half time. An in-play change takes effect at the next minute boundary, preserving the previous action's participants; half-time changes apply for the restart. Old events are never regenerated to make a substitution.

An inexpensive one-second manual sidecar saves positions, actions and the clock; major checkpoints, pause, goals, half time, leaving the screen and full time save the authoritative career/report. Reload pauses the recovered game until Resume. Interrupted sidecars fall back to the main checkpoint. A full manual contest cannot be committed before full time, and the ordinary one-time commit guard prevents repeated chemistry, growth or financial settlement. Finishing with the assistant explicitly preserves the played prefix and existing goals.

The career save schema/key, 546 custom starting footballers, club membership, free agents, promotion pool, finances, transfers, staff, legacy, achievements and existing game modes remain compatible. New fields are optional and validated. Historical box rows retain support for the previous compact format.
