# v2.1 — simulated matches with playable attacking highlights

Play Match now simulates the contest and pauses at key moments. Direct control appears only for up to two short attacking highlights: a breakaway (14 seconds) or open counterattack (18 seconds). These use a horizontal, field-level perspective with larger upright players, three attackers, two recovering defenders and a keeper. Portrait phones get a rotated landscape layout; turning the device sideways uses its natural landscape viewport.

Move with the left stick toward the goal on the right, pass into space with assisted receiver selection, and tap/hold Shoot. Moving up/down selects a goal corner. Teammates make forward support runs and control switches automatically on receiving a pass. Opponents recover through open space. Shooting distance, angle, aim, power, finishing, traits, position suitability, fitness, form, partnership familiarity and keeper ability affect the chance. Clear chances can score, be saved or miss; no input guarantees a goal. A missed highlight loses that opportunity, not the whole game.

Between highlights the regular match simulation continues. Corners, crosses and other big chances retain their three coaching options; penalties and free kicks retain drawn aiming. The start of a playable attack also offers **Use three coaching options** or **Let player decide**. Watch Match uses the coaching format throughout. Quick Sim remains available. A simple simulation/commentary view is the default between decisions; the optional animated overview remains under Match options.

## Results, substitutions and saves

The played attack replaces only its corresponding simulated chance, including the actual scorer, assist, save or failed delivery. Existing events and the other nine fixtures are preserved. Completed forward passes earn bounded partnership gains through the existing chemistry system. Later live substitutions preserve both the played result and those gains. Live Subs remain accessible between chances and planned/recent substitution plans remain supported.

Attacks pause on focus loss/backgrounding, save their movement, ball flight and random state, and reload paused. There is no reload reroll. Played chances can be settled only once; ordinary matchweek commit still happens at full time. Optional validated fields extend save schema 3 without changing its storage key. Existing full-contest manual saves remain playable and can use **Let assistant finish** to preserve the played score and simulate the rest; new matches use the hybrid flow.

## Original player models and research status

Original full-body vector models now appear in playable highlights, the optional match overview, legacy manual matches and player profiles. Portraits and bodies use the same persistent appearance reference. Traditional club shorts are separate from accent colors, with distinct goalkeeper strips. Sourced height changes visual proportions; it does not silently change collision physics or player ratings.

The appearance research is **incomplete**. All 546 custom identities are reconciled: 545 have sourced heights, 64 have individually inspected portrait references and five have published weight references (some historic). The other 482 custom appearances use explicit neutral placeholders. Mass/body shape remains unknown or an artistic interpretation, never a claimed measurement. The Championship pool and free-agent appearances still need individual references.
