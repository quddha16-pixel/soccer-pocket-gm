# v2.5 — Twenty picks. Your club.

The opening screen has two distinct modes: **Build Your Franchise**, the existing club-selection game, and **Draft Your Team**, a 20-round fantasy draft that creates a playable custom club. Existing regular careers retain their save and Continue button. A completed draft career has its own Continue button.

## The draft

Choose a team name (up to 32 characters), primary/secondary colors, GM name and one of the three existing managerial styles. Spin a club, browse its real custom squad, select a player and confirm the pick. Player cards show name, persistent illustration, age, natural position, overall, potential and existing traits. Position filters, balance counts and the 20-pick board support squad planning. Picks are final once confirmed; restarting a board is explicit.

The source is the existing **546-player custom Premier League roster**, including the owner's original player-to-club assignments. It does not use external current-club assignments, free agents or fictional replacements. Clubs can repeat on the spinner. A player can only be picked once. A spin is saved before its animation, has no reroll control, and survives refresh; a skip-animation control and reduced motion are supported. Candidate availability protects an XI at each source club and reserves unfilled squad requirements near the end.

The final roster has exactly **two goalkeepers and eighteen outfield players**, with at least one CM/WG/ST to support the existing designated-star feature. Other positional balance remains the GM's choice; the usual out-of-position penalties still apply. The club receives a real XI and bench drawn from those 20 selections.

Incomplete boards save separately after each spin/pick. Their small record stores the seed, identity choices and ordered selections. Validation replays the seeded sequence, rejects forged draws, wrong-club picks, duplicate players and invalid colors, and ensures every selection was eligible at that point.

## A separate football universe

The custom club has a unique draft ID, chosen colors, original vector badge, abbreviation, team-themed UI and home kit. Its founding strength is the actual starting XI's average rating. It enters the 2026/27 EPL in Sunderland's place. Sunderland and their remaining actual players/accounts move to the Championship pool; **Lincoln City leave that draft universe**. Lincoln have the lowest starting strength estimate in the existing pool (64). This is a game-data ranking, not a claim about a live Championship table.

There remain **20 EPL clubs, 24 pool clubs and 44 clubs overall**, with 380 league fixtures. The selected players move to the custom club using their existing IDs, abilities, traits, potential, contracts and appearance identities. Their source profiles remain intact as provenance. Unselected players remain attached to their existing clubs. All 546 original custom footballers are retained in the draft world; no duplicate identities are created. The separate 50-player free-agent pool remains available after career creation.

The new club receives staff, registration, familiarity, a legacy baseline and estimated accounts. Its starting transfer budget is £30m; estimated revenue/cash support the selected squad's existing payroll rather than silently placing it into immediate debt. No transfer fees, loan deals or fictional real-world financial references are invented for the draft allocations.

The new club participates in the existing Carabao/FA Cup field and can qualify for the Community Shield. League rules, promotion/relegation, coaching styles, market resistance, development, live/planned substitutions, drawn chances, awards, box scores and records use the same engines as regular careers. Club identity persists through season rollover and even if the GM later changes jobs.

## Save and data protection

- The regular key remains `pocket-football-gm-v1`; its schema version and match-saving cadence remain unchanged.
- Draft careers use `pocket-football-gm-fantasy-v1`, with their own playback/manual sidecars. An unfinished board uses `pocket-football-gm-draft-board-v1`.
- Starting or restarting a board does not delete a regular career or an existing completed draft career. An existing draft career is replaced only after a complete squad is reviewed and the in-game confirmation is accepted.
- The board is cleared only after the new draft career writes successfully. A failed career write leaves the board and regular save available.
- Save validation checks the optional mode/franchise metadata, all original player identities, the draft sequence and the world's allowed clubs. The custom club cannot replace a canonical club ID or palette, even if the user chooses a matching name.
- Canonical club arrays and player/research files remain intact. Context-specific club universes support the custom club across fixtures, cup validation, finances, legacy and promotions. Regular franchise worlds still contain their original clubs and colors.
