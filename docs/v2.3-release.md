# v2.3 — Watch, decide and chase trophies

The matchday experience is a management simulation again. Watch Match opens the animated broadcast by default; penalties, free kicks, corners, one-on-ones, crosses and big chances each offer three coaching approaches. Players execute the selected approach using the existing individual abilities and match engine. Direct player movement, shooting/pass buttons and new drawn shots are no longer offered. Quick Sim, planned/live substitutions, reusable substitution plans and all career management systems remain available.

## Broadcast and decisions

The stadium has seating, lighting, striped turf, nets and corner flags. All 22 footballers use illustrated full-body models with team kits, numbers, running strides and ground shadows. The ball moves through the existing passing/shot sequences with passing paths and a trail. Follow play changes the camera; Tactical view switches to clear numbered dots. Tap a footballer to follow their role, rating and fatigue. Playback controls and Substitutions stay together; portrait layouts reserve room for player details, with a compact strip on short screens.

Coaching results include an illustrative replay of the recorded outcome. Replaying does not resolve the chance again. Opponent results and unrelated chances remain unchanged by a user decision. The broadcast respects pause, visibility and reduced-motion preferences. Historical authored shots still replay, but new chances require no aiming input.

The underlying appearance research is unchanged: 545 of 546 custom players have sourced heights, 64 inspected portraits and five published weight references. Unverified appearance details remain placeholders; this release does not claim fully researched likenesses.

## Domestic competitions

Home → Road to Wembley and League → Cups show the Carabao Cup, FA Cup and Community Shield, with draws, schedules, results, shootouts, winners and past seasons. Cup ties support the same watching, decisions, substitutions and individual box scores as league matches. Archived cups retain compact box scores after season rollover. Player profiles separate current domestic cup statistics from league statistics.

This is a **custom 44-club cup field**, using the existing 20 league clubs and 24 real Championship-pool squads. It does not add the complete English football pyramid or reset transferred player ownership. Pool players, staff and accounts enter the world on first cup participation if they are not already present. The original 546 custom assignments and research files are unchanged.

| Competition | In-game structure |
| --- | --- |
| Carabao Cup | 24 pool clubs play the opening round; 20 EPL clubs enter the round of 32. Two-leg semi-finals, aggregate score with no away-goal rule, Wembley final. 45 fixtures. |
| FA Cup | Same custom opening field, single-match rounds, neutral Wembley semi-finals and final. 43 fixtures. |
| Community Shield | Previous in-game league winner versus FA Cup winner; a double sends the league runner-up. Begins after the first completed league/FA Cup campaign. |

Level knockout ties go **directly to simulated penalties**. Extra time and FA Cup replays are not implemented. Penalty probabilities use the actual takers and goalkeeper; shootout goals do not count in match or league scoring totals. The first Carabao semi-final leg can finish level without a declared winner.

Cup milestones follow league weeks: Carabao 2/5/9/16/22/24/28; FA Cup 3/7/14/23/31/36; Shield before league week one. AI-only rounds settle before the next scheduled league round. A user cup tie is played or watched before continuing the league calendar. League week, EPL points, Home win/draw/loss record and weekly payroll do not advance when a cup round is committed. Cup minutes, form, fatigue and familiarity are recorded. The existing development engine uses those minutes without an additional weekly growth settlement.

Gate/prize receipts are game estimates, recorded separately from transfer income: £150,000 per participating club (£250,000 at a neutral venue), round-winner prizes of £50,000 × stage number (starting at one) and £1m for a final winner. These figures are not represented as official English competition payments. Trophy legacy awards are FA Cup +250, Carabao +150, Shield +50, once per title, with three corresponding achievements.

## EPL title and save protection

The Premier League champion remains the club top after 38 matches, using the existing points, goal-difference and goals-scored standings. **No EPL playoffs were added.** Late-season panels show contenders and their remaining maximum points, live provisional standings, confirmed clinching and an end-of-season trophy presentation. Exact table ties retain the game's existing display fallback; official head-to-head/title-tiebreak procedures are outside this release.

New careers and existing week-zero careers receive cups immediately. Already active older seasons defer cups until the next season rather than inventing results. No historical inaugural Community Shield participants are invented.

The storage key, schema version and save-writing cadence remain unchanged. Optional cup records and round-specific playback identity extend the existing save format. Old direct-control checkpoints resume as watched simulations; already played goals, substitutions and unrelated fixtures are retained. Historical reports and authored shots remain readable. Original squads are not re-imported over established careers. AI-only cup rounds preserve a user's planned league substitutions.
