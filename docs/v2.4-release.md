# v2.4 — Matchday Moments

The five requested opportunities are now dedicated draw-to-play mini-games inside the watched simulation: penalties, free kicks, one-on-ones, corners and major crosses. Full-contest movement controls remain removed. Big play chances retain their three coaching decisions. The existing Interactive key moments preference, Quick Sim and delegation remain available.

## How the experience works

- **Penalty:** draw the route to goal, preview placement and power, then Strike. A chip finish uses the existing composure/keeper-commitment assessment.
- **Free kick:** shape a route around the actual wall region. A route through the wall can be blocked. Power, long shooting and set-piece ability affect execution.
- **One-on-one:** draw the finish and optionally chip the keeper. The keeper reacts and advances when the existing opportunity context calls for it.
- **Corner / cross:** first draw the delivery toward a near-post, far-post or cut-back receiving zone. Then draw the receiving player's finish and Play the chance. The scene cuts from the delivery camera to the goal camera. A failed delivery stops before the shot. The selected runner is the actual finisher in the match event, box score and ability assessment.

A stroke can be redrawn before confirmation, including returning to the delivery step. Swipe duration influences power. Driven/lofted delivery controls and driven/chip finishes change the applicable football assessment. There is no time limit to aim.

The dedicated stadium scene includes perspective goals and netting, seating, floodlights, turf, team kits, illustrated full-body footballers, target zones, a run-up/kick, reacting goalkeeper, ball trails and a result presentation. The mobile layout keeps the drawing ball directly touchable. Landscape replays use an adjacent result panel. The score ribbon reveals a chance's goal only after the replay completes.

Replays follow the **same bounded polyline that was displayed, saved and assessed**, including curved paths. Light smoothing and a maximum of 64 points apply when a stroke is released; the resulting preview is the confirmed route. Wall contacts, saves and intercepted deliveries stop along that route rather than sending the ball through the obstacle. Replay and slow motion do not run another football outcome calculation.

Optional synthesized sound starts only after user activation; supported devices can provide a brief impact vibration when sound is enabled. Sound defaults off and is not a career-save field. Slow motion, tap aiming, keyboard arrows/Shift to bend/Enter to set, pointer cancellation, redraw and reduced-motion playback are supported. Hidden documents pause the replay's clock. Let the players decide uses the same existing automatic football assessment.

This is original stylized SVG artwork and a game-specific trajectory/probability model. It is not a licensed broadcast, a photorealistic rendering system or a claim to outperform every commercial football game. Existing appearance research and honest placeholders remain unchanged.

## Football and save integration

The existing assessment still supplies player technique, traits, fatigue, fitness, natural-position suitability, form, opponents and keeper ability. The drawn layer adds goal placement, power, wall intersections and delivery accuracy. Crosses/corners assess the actual chosen receiver's attributes. A valid, well-placed stroke never guarantees a goal. Poor deliveries can be intercepted; an off-frame finish cannot be counted as a goal or save.

A confirmed `DrawnPlay` is an optional key-moment report field with a version, finish stroke, optional delivery stroke and flight. It is copied and validated before resolution. Both strokes and the result commit together. Reconfirming the same attempt is idempotent; an alternative attempt on an already resolved chance is rejected. Other chances and the nine unrelated fixtures remain unchanged. Later substitutions preserve completed attempts and only recalculate future play.

Drawing and replay use local UI state and direct SVG updates. They perform no career writes per pointer frame or animation frame. Only confirmation changes the career, using the existing save flow. A reload after confirmation restores the exact strokes and result. An unconfirmed drawing draft can be lost on refresh, without consuming or rerolling an attempt.

The save key, schema version, cadence, league progression, domestic calendars, contracts, finances, development, awards and original 546 custom assignments remain intact. Legacy authored-shot reports still use their historical replay. Old player-control checkpoints continue through the existing safe recovery into the watched timeline and receive the new UI when a relevant unresolved chance appears. Current and archived cup compatibility is retained.
