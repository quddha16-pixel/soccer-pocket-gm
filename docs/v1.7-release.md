# v1.7 — competitive clubs, substitutions and positional discipline

The transfer market previously accepted any fee meeting an asking price, with only basic squad numbers checked at completion. This allowed the user to strip competitors of important players. Clubs now have a sporting veto, shared by human and AI buyers.

## Competitive transfers

- Healthy registered first-team selections determine key players and replacement quality, independent of club reputation.
- Clubs protect fit positional cover, long-contract core players and their ability to replace starters before the deadline. Direct rivals pay a premium and cannot simply buy protected key players. Winter and accumulated departures increase resistance.
- A club can refuse any fee. Negotiator discounts apply to negotiable prices, never override a sporting veto. Expiring contracts, financial pressure, equivalent cover and intentional transfer listings can make appropriate deals possible.
- Maximum four departures and two first-team departures per club per window; a persistent ledger protects this even after older offers are removed. Existing saves recover completed sales from available history. Recently signed players cannot immediately be flipped for six matchweeks.
- Agreements expire after two matchweeks or a window change. Completion rechecks ownership, cover, seller willingness, price and existing financial/registration rules. The market provides **Reopen club talks** to obtain a fresh agreement.
- AI clubs assess their weakest first-team position each window week, consider several alternatives when their first choice is unavailable and face the same seller, player, cash, wage and registration checks. Their sporting valuation caps bids on user listings, including completion of older incoming offers.
- The current market supports permanent transfers. This release does not introduce a separate loan-contract system.

## Substitutions

**Matchday → Set your subs** chooses up to nine fit registered bench players and timed instructions for Watch Match or Quick Sim. Five players may be substituted at three distinct stoppages; minute 45 represents an additional half-time window. Instructions use minutes 1–89. Changes at minute 60 take effect from minute 61. A player cannot return after leaving, and a goalkeeper must occupy the goalkeeper slot.

**Make a substitution** pauses live playback. A change preserves every already watched event and the other nine fixtures, and resolves the remaining play with the new personnel. Future instructions can be cancelled. Repeating the same planned/manual change at the same minute produces the same report. Playback speed, refresh and skipping to full time do not reroll results. AI sides also choose benches and make suitable changes.

The pitch and commentary show the substitution timeline. Actual minutes drive development, fatigue, performance ratings, appearances, keeper saves/concessions and overlapping partnership familiarity. There are exactly 990 player-minutes per club per completed match; unused substitutes receive no appearance. Clean sheets require at least 60 minutes without conceding while on the pitch. Plans reset after the matchweek is committed, so next week's instructions do not silently repeat.

## Position effectiveness

| Familiarity | Effective contribution |
| --- | ---: |
| Primary position | 100% |
| Listed secondary position | 98% |
| Closely related role | 88% |
| Unfamiliar role in the same position group | 72% |
| Unfamiliar attacking/midfield role | 60% |
| Unrelated defensive/attacking role | 40% |

Legacy players without detailed real profiles use corresponding conservative coarse-position penalties. Goalkeeper/outfield mismatches remain blocked by lineup/substitution validation.

The engine uses occupied formation slots when selecting attackers, creators, carriers and defenders. Role familiarity directly reduces their contributions and finishing; a striker placed in defence no longer contributes as a natural striker while also occupying a defender's slot. The same rules apply to substitutes and AI lineups. Pitch badges show effective positional ratings and unfamiliar-role highlights; selection sheets and substitution controls disclose suitability. Underlying OVR and research attributes are preserved. Strong teams can still win with a poor selection; this is a probability model, not a forced-loss rule.

## Save and data protection

Schema 3 and the existing storage key remain unchanged. New plan, timeline and transfer-ledger fields are optional. Historical results are retained, legacy 22-player reports remain readable, and a legacy pending match can gain a bench while preserving its watched events. Existing squad assignments, player identities, researched profiles and previously completed transfers are not rewritten.

The original 546-player custom roster, draft, owner submission and staged FC 27 research files are unchanged. Championship profiles and free-agent research are also unchanged. Work is committed to `work`; `main` is preserved.
