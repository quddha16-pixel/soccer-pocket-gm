# v1.8 — interactive key moments

Watched matches can now stop for the managed club's key attacking decisions. The pause occurs before the chance's shot, goal or failed delivery is shown. A saved result panel explains the outcome, then **Continue match** resumes play. Opponents make their own decisions; the user never chooses their opponent's attack.

## Situations and actions

| Situation | Action 1 | Action 2 | Action 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Penalty | Low, hard power shot to one side | Precise placement into the opposite corner | Panenka down the middle |
| Free kick | Direct curl/power shot | Floated cross | Short pass for a better angle |
| Corner | Inswinger to the near post | Outswinger to the far post/penalty spot | Short corner |
| One on one | Early low shot | Dribble around/nutmeg the keeper | Chip the advancing keeper |
| Big play | First-time shot | Touch and shoot | Square to a better-placed teammate |
| Major cross | Whipped high ball to the far post | Driven ball through the six-yard box | Cut-back to the edge of the area |

Each action has a readable tradeoff, the current player and an opposition read. Small screens use shorter hints so all three actions fit together. Larger screens also show an original schematic pitch illustration. The interface supports keyboard navigation, expanded match view and reduced-motion text playback.

## Football model

The existing chance engine generates these opportunities; it does not force all six situations into each match. Non-penalty decision opportunities are limited to seven per club per match to keep playback manageable; penalties are retained beyond that limit. This is a compact match model, not a full event-by-event referee or foul simulation.

Abilities, fatigue, form, positional fit, opposition quality, pressure and keeper movement affect outcomes. Penalty shots use finishing, shot power, control and composure against the goalkeeper. Free kicks use range, power, delivery and set-piece traits. Crosses and corners match the delivery with aerial or finishing targets; short options use passing/control and an appropriate supporting player. Dribbles around a keeper can fail before a shot. Touching the ball costs time under pressure. A chip or Panenka is less attractive against a keeper who stays set.

Delivery/dribble completion and shot conversion are separate probability checks. Failed moves record no phantom shot, save or assist. Passing options can change the actual shooter and award the delivery player an assist. A penalty has its own conversion range, capped below certainty, rather than the open-play 48% cap. These are original game assessments, not claims about sourced FC ratings or official scoring probabilities. No action guarantees success.

Quick Sim, uninterrupted watching, opponents and delegated chances assess the same three approaches and use the same outcome rules. A small deterministic tactical preference avoids identical decisions for every situation. The existing Feed Your Star tactic still influences the intended receiver; coaching styles, chemistry and occupied-position penalties remain relevant.

## Replay, substitutions and saves

- **Interactive key moments** is enabled by default before Watch Match. Turning it off retains automatic playback. **Let player decide**, closing a choice sheet and skipping to full time delegate the relevant remaining decision(s).
- Choosing changes only that chance. Already played events, unrelated chances and the other nine fixtures retain their outcomes. Source-interval event IDs keep later chance identities stable even when an earlier delivery produces no shot.
- Choices lock after submission. Repeating the same submission is idempotent; choosing a different action for an already selected chance is rejected. Reloading preserves waiting choices and result sheets rather than supplying a new outcome.
- Two chances in the same minute resolve before advancing that minute. The 90th-minute chance resolves before the full-time screen.
- Manual and planned substitutions update future participants and chances. Completed choices and watched events remain intact. A selected result must be continued before substitutions can alter the remaining play.
- Scores, shots, goals, assists, keeper saves/concessions, ratings and Man of the Match reconcile from the resulting events. Existing season awards and records consume these final reports without a separate scoring ledger.
- Schema 3 and the existing save key remain unchanged. New preferences, moments and pending-decision fields are optional. Historical reports and older pending matches remain readable; an old pending match continues normally, with interactive moments available from the next newly prepared matchweek.
- All custom roster, Championship and free-agent research files remain unchanged. Development stays on `work`; `main` is preserved.

## Easier substitution access

Live matches now have a sticky **Substitutions** button immediately beside the playback controls, including expanded match view. The pitch leaves room for it above mobile navigation. The sheet opens paused, puts the player-off/player-on selectors first, and keeps **Confirm substitution** and **Done** visible in a fixed footer. The optional **Browse bench** section offers selectable player cards instead of placing a long passive bench list before the controls.

The former disabled-at-minute-zero case is handled as a kickoff lineup change: swapping a bench player into the starting XI before the first minute updates the starting team, bench, designated star and any scheduled identity swaps, without consuming a substitution or crediting the replaced player with playing time. After kickoff, the usual five-player/three-window rules apply. **Clear future substitution plans** releases bench players reserved for later instructions so the user can make the remaining changes manually. Completed substitutions and opponent instructions remain intact.

Quick Sim applies changes scheduled under **Set your subs** before kickoff; live manual changes are available while watching. Returning from the sheet leaves playback paused until the user presses Resume.
