# v2.2 — live pitch, playable corners and responsive field controls

## Match viewing and routing

The animated mini-pitch is the default again. All 22 active players have circular illustrated markers. Continuous seeded possession sequences show moving carriers, visible pass flights, turnovers, support runs, defensive movement and keeper adjustments between recorded chances. Pass recipients become the next passers; the last shot no longer freezes the ball in the goal. The presentation follows the actual lineup and substitutions. Match results and box-score statistics continue to use the authoritative match report.

In **Play Match**, one-on-ones open horizontal field play. Crosses and other big chances retain their three coaching options. Corners open horizontal play for both teams: take your deliveries and defend the opposition's. **Watch Match** retains coaching/delegation, and **Quick Sim** remains available. Reduced-motion preferences and pause are respected.

## Corners

New simulated games schedule **4–17 corners total**, with a stable target and event IDs. Corner totals appear in match statistics. Live substitutions preserve completed corners and their outcomes, while future participants use the updated lineup. Old recorded results remain intact.

Your corner begins with a real taker at the flag. Aim up/down for near/far post, choose **Cross** or **Short**, then control the receiver. Shoot during delivery to buffer a first-time header. The lofted ball arrives at head height; heading, jumping, composure and keeper skill affect the finish. Defence and interception outcomes remain possible.

For an opponent corner, move a defender, **Switch** to another marker and time **Clear** as the delivery drops. A clearance closes the chance without a phantom shot. Opponent goals and successful clearances receive appropriate feedback. Delegation remains available. Played results settle once and preserve the other nine fixtures.

## Draw-to-shoot

Penalties and free kicks retain drawn paths, tap/keyboard aiming, redraw and confirmation. Stroke duration adds power control. The preview shows power, placement and wall risk. The scene adds a stadium, larger original player models, an identity-matched defensive wall, luminous paths, a spinning ball and flight trail, keeper dives, net response and impact effects. The ball follows the stored path, including the wall-contact point on a block. Goal effects follow the actual result. Reduced-motion replay remains supported.

## One-on-ones and opportunity frequency

All natural one-on-one shots are eligible for field play rather than sometimes hidden by a second chance-kind roll. The non-corner decision cap rises from seven to ten per team, more crosses are surfaced, and more close chances offer coaching choices. This exposes existing match-engine chances rather than manufacturing extra guaranteed goals. Match results remain probabilistic.

The keeper advances to narrow the angle and can smother at the attacker’s feet. The aiming line shows the selected goal target. Placement, distance, angle, beating the keeper and individual attributes influence the finish. Choose Low or Chip with a compact toggle: chips have higher loft and can punish an advancing keeper but are less effective against a keeper who holds the line. Support passes still transfer player control and build the existing capped partnership credit.

## Club access and legacy opportunity graphics

Established managers receive 17 of the 20 starting-club offers, including all smaller rebuilding clubs and European challengers. The three title favourites require elite standing. Elite managers retain all-club access. The same policy applies to season-boundary offers, while an incumbent can stay and successful seasons still earn reputation and poaching approaches.

Every starting-club and season-boundary job card shows a comparable stacked legacy-points graphic, title total, fourth-place and seventeenth-place outcomes, plus an accessible breakdown. Forecasts use the same placement-scoring function as actual settlement, include genuine first-title/rebuild eligibility and exclude repeat first-title bonuses. Signing itself earns no points. Next-season targets are labelled previews until rollover; awards, finances and other seasonal bonuses are separate.

## Movement circle

The horizontal-play stick now updates its knob directly from touch, with cached coordinate mapping for rotated layouts, a small anti-jitter centre and comfortable full travel. Input feeds a 120 Hz simulation step. Actor and ball positions, aim guides and flight trails are presented each display frame through cached scene nodes; the pitch no longer rebuilds its full SVG scene for continuous movement. Secondary UI updates have a lower cadence, with compositor-driven running strides. The camera stays stable during a chance, running poses reflect actual movement, and redundant background blur is removed. Pointer-specific release/cancellation prevents a second finger from clearing a held movement input.

**Career save writing, storage key/version, save frequency and career progression are unchanged.** The original one-second highlight checkpoint remains. Loader validation accepts the new corner gameplay records. Interrupted v2.1 counters remain playable, including a same-minute opposition corner.
