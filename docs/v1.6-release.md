# Pocket Football GM v1.6 — implementation and research

Created by Quentin Warminsky. Development branch: `work`. Existing `main`, original 546-player snapshots, custom club assignments and branch history are preserved. This release adds to the existing simulator.

## Playable features

- All 20 starting clubs appear in the contract grid. Unknown reputation highlights six available clubs; Established and Elite highlight all 20. Locked clubs remain visible and cannot be selected through the API.
- Three saved managerial specialties: Developer (positive growth ×1.2 before existing caps), Negotiator (10% buying-ask reduction, 12% base wage reduction, reduced star ambition premium, three-week minimum bonus and lower anchored renewal demand), and Football genius (exclusive coached tactical patterns). Bonuses belong to the user’s club and do not leak into AI transactions. Game finances and registration remain binding.
- Genius-only Positional mastery, Coordinated counterpress and Disguised transitions affect chance creation, carrying, defensive pressure, one-on-one conversion and fitness. Every pattern has a cost or tactical tradeoff. Other specialties have no access or simulation benefit from these patterns.
- Real-world home colors for all 44 clubs, original two-color crests, managed-club themes, accessible accents and readable match-token numbers. Color clashes receive a contrasting token kit. This does not claim official licensed shirt designs or club brand certification.
- Real head-coach names for the original 20 clubs, visible in staff and the league directory. Initial named coaches include the user-selected club. The entered career name remains intact. Existing save staff identities and future in-game dismissals are preserved; fictional AI replacements lose the sourced-name reference.
- Fifty real unattached-player reference profiles in new careers. Previous careers can import the pool explicitly from Market, once, without replacing existing players or reclaiming transferred/retired identities. Future imports age players and respect the game’s retirement rule.
- Eight season awards: in-game Ballon d’Or, Player of the Season, Golden Boot, Playmaker, Golden Glove, Yashin Trophy, Kopa Trophy and Chance Creator. Only committed simulated league matches count. There is no invented worldwide competition or voting data. Position-specific contribution, sustained ratings, appearances and team achievement enter the main award; no OVR/fame shortcut is used. Counting awards share ties; other ties use appearances and stable identity for reproducibility. Kopa eligibility uses game age at season start (21 or younger).
- Season and all-time player totals, individual single-match bests and club records. Records survive transfers, season changes, retirement and refresh. Goalkeeper clean sheets and saves are derived from recorded performances; chance creation derives from recorded shot creator IDs. Team streaks cross consecutive seasons but reset when the club leaves the league.

## Free-agent methodology and limits

The pool is a curated selection of 50 researched professionals across positions and nationalities, ranked in-game by their assessed ability. It is not claimed as a certified exhaustive worldwide top-50 ranking.

Each selected identity has a birth date, positional reference and reported unattached-status source checked on **28 October 2026**. A player’s status can change after publication; the game imports a dated snapshot into the custom 2026/27 starting universe rather than running a live transfer feed. Starting availability dates, sterling values, wages, contract assumptions, potential and development remain game estimates.

**1 source-rated FC 27 overall/profile (Karim Benzema), 49 provisional overall/attribute assessments.** Many unattached players have no current official EA entry. Missing entries were not replaced with a generic 70: each provisional assessment specifies an individual overall, six assessment categories, detailed strengths/limitations, explicit skill adjustments and justified traits or no-trait rationale. Keeper skills use keeper-specific categories. Ratings are deterministic game judgments, never labelled official FC 27 values. Foot and secondary-position assumptions are marked estimated where the selected evidence does not verify them.

Selected factual inputs and original assessment rationale are in `docs/research/free-agent-facts.json`; compiled playable snapshots are in `src/data/free-agents.json`. Rebuild with `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python scripts/build-free-agents.py`. The original custom 546 and staged 664 Championship profiles are not modified. Combined real identity catalog: **1,260**, with **596** real players instantiated in a fresh career before any Championship club enters.

The first candidate list was rejected because its free-agent labels included players with newer contracted clubs. Excluded examples include Tijjani Reijnders ([Saudi Pro League signing](https://www.spl.com.sa/en/news/al-qadsiah-bolster-ranks-by-bringing-reijnders-from-manchester-city)), Lutsharel Geertruida ([PSV loan announcement](https://www.psv.nl/es/media/artikel/transfer-lutsharel-geertruida-op-huurbasis-naar-psv)), Dani Carvajal ([Getafe signing](https://www.reuters.com/sports/soccer/former-real-madrid-defender-carvajal-signs-getafe-2026-10-13/)), Riyad Mahrez ([Al Shamal announcement](https://qna.org.qa/en/news/news-details?date=14%2F10%2F2026&id=al-shamal-sign-algeria-star-riyad-mahrez)), and Will Smallbone ([Leicester announcement](https://www.lcfc.com/media-article/will-smallbone-new-leicester-city-signing-press-release-lcfc)). Alaba, Neto, Coutinho, Wijnaldum, Bissouma, Malacia, Goretzka and others with newer club evidence were also left out. Expiring contracts alone were not treated as proof of free agency.

Official EA pages were used where accessible. Empty results/404 profiles were not treated as verified data. Direct requests to Transfermarkt (405), Retained List/Wikipedia/MercatoIndex (403) were not retried through alternate proxies. Permitted indexed references supplied selected identity/status facts where available. No vendor descriptions, photos, badges or complete proprietary database were bundled.

## Coaches and colors

The starting 20 names are supported by the [Premier League’s 2026/27 managerial line-up](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4679012/meet-the-202627-premier-league-clubs-managers), with dated factual records in `docs/research/managers-2026.json`. Staff ages, wages, contracts, rating and specialist ability are estimates rather than researched biographical/financial claims.

Traditional colors were checked against George Chilvers’s [RSSSF England team-colors reference](https://www.rsssf.org/colours/england.html), [Football-Stadiums kit-color guide](https://www.football-stadiums.co.uk/articles/football-club-kit-colours/) and [EFL color reference](https://www.apparelnbags.com/teamcolors/efl.htm). Liverpool’s primary red follows its [published brand guide](https://assets.liverpoolfc.com/uploads/LFCBrandGuidelines_May2016.pdf). The older kit references establish traditional colors, not the precise design of a 2026/27 shirt. RGB choices are game rendering approximations; the accessible action color is deliberately lightened when a primary would be unreadable on the dark background.

## Save and statistics protection

Schema/key remain version 3 / `pocket-football-gm-v1`. Optional specialty fields leave older saves neutral. Current original rosters are not retroactively overwritten. Pool import is explicit and idempotent. Archived reviews store compact per-player totals, best-match records and awards before old detailed reports are removed. The current season is not counted again when an archived review exists. No pending/uncommitted match counts toward awards or records.

Archives created before this feature retain team scores but often lack individual reports. Those seasons contribute team records only. Missing player goals, assists, saves, chance creation and award winners are not reconstructed. UI coverage notes make this limitation visible.

## Validation

114 tests cover the existing game and the new offer grid, reference integrity, real free-agent deals, imports and legacy protection; specialties, growth, terms/affordability, renewal discount anchoring, AI isolation, real match probabilities and fitness; award eligibility/ties/role scoring; exact event-derived counts; archived totals, best games, transfers, streaks and refresh. Existing three-season, six-rollover and long-term development checks remain.

Production TypeScript/Vite build passes, with the existing advisory for a single data-heavy bundle (about 343 KB gzip). `scripts/mobile-qa.cjs` and `scripts/expansion-qa.cjs` pass at 320×568, 375×812, 390×844 and 430×932. New browser checks include actual career creation, a full 38-week simulation, end-of-season ceremony, archive browsing, promotion rollover and saved-style/record restoration. These are Chromium mobile-emulation checks rather than physical iPhone/Safari tests.

The existing GitHub Pages workflow remains the publishing path; no merge or force push is used. The final chat report identifies the deployed commit and live verification.
