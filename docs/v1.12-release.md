# v1.12 — individual box scores for every game

Completed matches now have a Box score action in each matchweek result and in League → Fixtures. Full-time Matchday also offers View match box score before committing the round. Any of the ten matches can be reviewed; closing the box returns to the results dialog or fixture list without changing results or advancing the week.

## One team at a time

The top menu has the two club insignias. Tap either to select that team; the tabs support keyboard arrows, Home and End, and remain visible while scrolling. Switching resets the player expansion and scroll position. Only the selected team's list is rendered.

The main list shows name, assigned role, starter/substitute status, minutes, goals, assists and rating. Tap a player to expand shots, shots on target, chances created, saves, tackles and goals conceded, plus the exact interval played. Profiles remain accessible from the expanded row. A star marks Player of the Match. Named unused substitutes show zero minutes and no match rating, and receive no invented appearance.

Expandable team totals show possession, shots, shots on target, chances created, saves and tackles. Player stats come from that match's recorded performances and events. Chances created follow the game's existing definition: a pass leading to a recorded shot, including an assist. Goalkeeper saves and conceded goals use the interval the goalkeeper actually played. Football statistics the engine does not record, such as passing accuracy or cards, are not fabricated.

## Historical retention

Season rollover now archives compact per-game box rows beside the existing historical scores. These retain all participants and named substitutes, positions, minutes/intervals, goals, assists, shots, shots on target, chances, saves, tackles, conceded goals, ratings, possession and Player of the Match. Replay event timelines remain omitted from historical seasons to keep the archive smaller. Completed archives stay read-only during financial, transfer and staff updates; live state is copied independently so each transaction does not recopy every historical match. Shared player identities supply names and persistent portraits; transferred and retired players' records remain associated with their original match team.

The season selector distinguishes repeated fixture IDs across years. Finished games remain reviewable after refresh, club changes and season rollover. Existing active-season reports become available immediately. Older archived games whose reports were discarded before this release retain their scores and show an explicit Individual stats unavailable message. No player stats or bench lists are reconstructed from current squads.

Unfinished games expose no box-score action, and pending simulation results stay hidden until the playback clock reaches full time. The save schema and browser key are unchanged; the historical box archive is optional. Validation checks fixture references, known/unique players, bounded stats, exact minute totals, goal totals, valid ratings and unused-substitute records.
